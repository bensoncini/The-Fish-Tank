The Fish Tank

Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

The Fish Tank is a private reading list for a small group of friends. It isn't a business, has no ads, and doesn't sell or share anyone's information.

What we store

  • From your Google sign-in: your email address and an account ID. Firebase also keeps the name and profile picture from your Google account with your sign-in record, but the site doesn't display or use them.
  • Your fish: the fish name and colour you pick.
  • Your books: titles, authors, which shelf they're on, ratings, notes, and the dates you added or finished them.

Who can see it

  • Only people whose email is on the group's member list can see the shelves. Everyone on that list sees everyone's books, fish names, notes and ratings.
  • Your email address isn't shown on the site. The person who runs the tank can see members' emails in the Firebase console, because that's where the member list is kept.

Where it's kept

Sign-in and storage are handled by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Google's and GitHub's own privacy policies apply to those services.

Other services the site talks to

  • Open Library (openlibrary.org, run by the Internet Archive): when you search for a book, what you type is sent to Open Library, and cover images load from their servers. Your email and fish details are never sent.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's typefaces.

The site uses no analytics or tracking. It stores sign-in state in your browser so you stay signed in.

Removing your information

  • You can remove any of your own books at any time with the “Release” button.
  • To remove your fish and email from the tank entirely, ask the person who runs it. They'll delete your fish and take your email off the member list.
